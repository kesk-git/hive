# Synapse workflow contract

Owner: `synapse`. This file carries the behavioural obligations and acceptance cases of the design's [local gate](../docs/design.md#synapse-on-the-operators-machine), for stage 5's plugin route and the later native route. It adds no executable or record schema; stage 5 defines those interfaces. `test-synapse` proves the cases against recorded executors, with route qualification separately proving the installed plugin's capabilities.

## Reviewer evidence

Synapse has one reviewer per review round and one fixer, selected by the [dispatch matrix's gate entry](dispatch.md#dispatch-matrix): `reviewer`, `reviewer_after_round` with its `after_round`, `fixer` and `fixer_after_round` with its `after_round`. The reviewer runs on the operator's machine in a separate session from the implementation Swarm. Its one result per round retains reviewer identity, actual model where verifiable, session receipt, policy version, base/head, verdict and complete findings in the ledger, in the shape of the design's [Checks and review contracts](../docs/design.md#checks-and-review-contracts). A missing or silently substituted result cannot pass. Readiness, candidate publication and Assimilation require that result for the current head, including only the verified round-3 continuity below, together with the other gate obligations.

The reviewer's first-choice counterpart is from another vendor, following the matrix's rule for every class. It is taken only under a recorded temporary rule or when the first choice is exhausted, with the resolved selection and its reason recorded. It fills the one reviewer role; it adds no second review of the head. A second look after a fix is the next review round, subject to the continuity rule below. The scheduled full run of main has no review step: it runs tests and reports.

Prove that one selected reviewer result satisfies the review obligation; a fix requiring new-head review starts the next round with one reviewer; a configured after-round reviewer transition and an authorized different-vendor counterpart each retain one role and the selection receipt; a missing result or silent substitution holds the gate; and a scheduled full run of main launches tests and reporting with no reviewer.

## Review rounds and rulings

The review and fix loop has one fix writer. Review rounds are uncapped. From round 3, review covers the delta since the last reviewed head and any production-correctness or security defect anywhere; coverage, wording, simplification and documentation findings become Hunt notes. From that round, an approve with zero blockers ends the review loop unless the fix touched production code, which requires review at its new head. For a non-production fix after a zero-blocker approval, the gate records current-head continuity to that approval only after verifying that the intervening fix changed no production code. Required checks and the other readiness obligations still stand; an unreviewed production change never inherits an approval. Fix-turn budgets and escalation can hold or park work but cannot make a defect pass or impose a review-round cap.

A ruling is the finding plus one sentence. A fixer may skip a finding it cannot place with confidence, must say so in its turn, and leaves it unresolved rather than claiming it fixed. Ask-user findings still require the complete owning-supervisor disposition; neither uncertainty nor a declined finding supplies missing review evidence.

The [test contract](test-targets.md#acceptance-proof-and-pre-checks) owns acceptance-test-first, scenario coverage and proof-of-record placement. No workflow rule has a freeze window; the Hive Mind may amend it whenever it says.

Prove a third-round review finds a production defect outside the delta while filing coverage and wording as notes; a zero-blocker approval ends after a non-production fix but a production fix receives new-head review; further required rounds remain possible beyond the escalation signal; and one-sentence rulings and explicit uncertain skips keep their original findings and unresolved dispositions.

## Plugin qualification

**Read-only plugin qualification.** Before selecting a plugin route, perform a read-only version and capability handshake against the installed executor. An incompatible API holds the route with a named upgrade block owned by the supervisor; Hive does not update or restart a shared daemon, rewrite global settings or fall back destructively to make the route pass. Version text without the required capability evidence is insufficient. Acceptance: an older executor lacking a required capability reports the owning supervisor and exact gap before launch, while global files, daemon and active runs remain unchanged; a compatible executor qualifies without a write. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1445](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1445).

**Immutable effective review configuration.** For each plugin run, qualify the effective gate entry's reviewer binding, configured after-round transition and authorized counterpart selection through supported launch capabilities or a verified configuration snapshot that remains immutable for that run. Global preflight alone does not bind the reviewer a later round will actually run. If a launch override substitutes a reviewer outside that binding or the supported API cannot preserve it, hold the route rather than infer it. Acceptance: change shared configuration between preflight and launch, and exercise an override that replaces the selected agent; neither can start a qualified run on a silently substituted reviewer, while a supported immutable binding retains its evidence through the run and selects exactly one reviewer in each round. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1434](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1434).

## Checks and responses

**Complete result and actual edits.** A complete plugin result that explicitly records no changes is a valid no-op, including a zero-finding result with an empty summary; a missing or incomplete result is unknown. Determine edits from the before/after head, index and worktree as well as the result, so a fixer that commits its own edits cannot hide a head change behind a clean working tree. Changed work requires the applicable new-head evidence. Acceptance: a complete zero-finding, no-edit result with an empty summary completes without inventing edits, a missing result holds, and a self-committed fix with a clean worktree invalidates the old head’s affected proof. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/pull/1431](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/pull/1431).

**Atomic gate response.** A plugin response binds the run, round, head and finding-set revision and is checked atomically when accepted. A stale binding changes nothing. Read-then-respond is not sufficient: if the supported plugin API cannot enforce this binding, the route stays held. Acceptance: move the gate after collection but before response, independently changing each binding; each stale response is rejected without fixing, advancing or disposing any finding, and a plugin without atomic binding cannot qualify. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1414](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1414).

**Required proof before downstream work.** Reject a transition that cannot produce a required review or check before launching downstream tests, documentation work or publication. Declining one finding with a recorded authorized disposition does not omit its reviewer’s required result or waive another proof obligation. Acceptance: a response that skips a required review is held before any downstream executor spends work, while a complete review containing one properly declined finding retains that review and all other obligations. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1441](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1441).

**Exit, result and output drain.** The execution boundary records process termination, protocol-result completeness and output-drain outcome separately. A complete bound result is preserved when a descendant holds output open; bounded drain and descendant cleanup report the transport problem without discarding completed evidence. Missing, partial or contradictory results remain unknown, and an exit code alone cannot supply them. Acceptance: a child emits a complete result and exits while a descendant retains an output handle; drain and owned-descendant cleanup finish within bounds and retain the result with a drain-failure receipt; partial output and an incompatible exit/result pair never pass. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1444](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1444).

## Fix preparation

**Role-specific repair preparation.** Prepare the environment required by each of the reviewer, fixer and verification roles explicitly before that role runs. A missing verification tool does not erase an authorized edit or claim that it was tested: the repair receipt records edits made, verification unavailable and readiness blocked separately. The sole fix owner remains responsible for scope and preserves its work while the owning supervisor resolves an environment block. Acceptance: a fixer can make its authorized correction while a required test tool is unavailable; the resulting receipt identifies the changed head and unperformed check, holds readiness and never reports a pass or rolls the edit away. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1428](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1428).

## Publication and Assimilation

**Bounded publication and uncertain push.** Publication bounds connection establishment, transfer inactivity and total operation time, including its retries; Shadow read bounds alone do not bound a push. After an uncertain push, reconcile the exact remote branch and expected head before retrying. Keep still-valid head-bound checks and reviews while delivery is unresolved; a transport failure alone does not invalidate them. Acceptance: a stalled connection, stalled transfer and repeated slow progress each reach their configured total bound; a push accepted remotely before the client loses its reply is reconciled without a duplicate mutation, and its valid head proof is reused. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1432](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1432).

**Public transport representation.** Normalize generated public text before hashing and sending, including terminal control sequences and the transport’s newline representation, then compare the published read-back in that same actual representation. Preserve the original proof privately subject to Secrets; public normalization never rewrites canonical operator words or private proof. Acceptance: generated text containing control sequences and mixed newlines produces one stable sent digest and matching forge read-back, while the original private evidence remains intact and a changed public body is detected. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1435](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1435).

**Green is not merge eligibility.** The merge-eligibility observation names the current blocking condition and any required base update, separately from green checks and Synapse readiness. The branch’s custody owner performs a bounded authorized repair and revalidation, or hands the owning supervisor a verified stop or explicit withholding reason. Unknown eligibility permits only bounded observation; no ineligible state appears as an indefinite successful wait. Acceptance: green checks on a head behind a base that requires updating produce the named base update and a custody-owned repair or verified withholding handoff; an unrepairable conflict and prolonged unknown state stay visibly blocked, and only verified eligible state reaches the guarded merge. Sources: [upstream no-mistakes/pull/1404](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/pull/1404), [upstream no-mistakes/issues/1402](https://github.com/kunchenguid/no-mistakes/issues/1402).

**The landing that completes a Target.** Assimilation decides whether a landing completes its Target when it lands, never from the readiness its Hunt reached while a sibling was still open. Under one landing boundary shared by every landing beneath the Target, on the Synapse and the direct route alike, it re-reads the completion of every Prey and Hunt beneath the Target and every in-flight landing receipt there before the merge; the task/authority lock alone serializes no sibling. The landing that would leave nothing beneath the Target open carries the Target's long tests and any remote proof bound at its head, by its route, and is withheld until its head has that proof. An uncertain landing receipt, including a landing read at its destination with no Pool completion recorded after it, is reconciled first from the destination its delivery lands on, the forge for a Harvest delivery and the local destination branch for a local-only delivery, which has no forge identity, and no other landing beneath that Target lands until it is. No Target worker and no finalizer is added; the boundary's and the receipt's representation is stage 5's. Acceptance: two last siblings ready at once, on each route, and a crash between a landing and its Pool completion, on the forge and on a local-only project's destination branch, each withhold exactly the completing landing until its head has the Target's proof, while the other lands as an ordinary Hunt, as the design's [Assimilation](../docs/design.md#assimilation-on-the-operators-machine) states.
