# Version 1.19

Version 1.19 defines the decision intake of stage 2, the `word` package, in [word.md](../word.md), with the records and operations of the Pool it persists through.

A question the Hive Mind owes holds one item through one `word` hold: the work it gates, or a decision item the Pool adds for a question with its own scope. It is raised with `hive-word hold`, corrected in place with `revise`, and answered through `answer`, the one intake, which records the exact words with the source they arrived by, a review submission, a session input or an operator request, and asks no proof of presence. An accepted answer never releases the hold: the owning supervisor records its disposition with a note of its own, `release`, which frees the work as the answer directs, or `complete`, which releases no work. Later records a deferral with its revisit date and is never approval. An answer given against an earlier revision of the question, a Later included, is recorded exactly as submitted, unreconciled, and applied to nothing. Answers are deduplicated by their question and source, an applied answer is replaced only by an answer that names it while its disposition is pending, and an answer entry is never changed once written. A question that has become moot is closed with `close` on evidence the Pool checks, the held work archived or the question asked again as another question that holds the same item, and never as an answer; a question and a card have no superseded state. `verify` and the rule of [The word stands](../word.md#the-word-stands) answer whether the Mind's word stands, from the record and bound to the question revision the caller read, and the Pool applies the same rule where `hive-admit approve`, the budget record and a project's posture rule accept a `word` authority.

The questions and decision rounds are Pool records, `pool.question` and `pool.decision-round`, written by six new operations of `hive-pool`, `hold-question`, `revise-question`, `answer-question`, `release-question`, `complete-question` and `close-question`, which accept only a handoff from `hive-word`. Every check that reads a record as it stands is the Pool's, inside the transaction that writes. [commands.md](../commands.md#a-command-with-no-store) states how a command with no store replays through the store it persists through: it reads its recorded outcome first through the callee's read of kind `request-outcome`, which `hive-pool read` now offers with the kinds `question` and `decision-round`, then sends one handoff whose replay the callee keys on the original request's digest. [pool.md](../pool.md) adds the store paths, the release of a `word` hold by a disposition or a moot close, the binding of an imported `word` hold to the first question raised on its item, the `word` authority verified by admission, nine receipt kinds, seven event kinds, thirteen error codes and the proofs of `test-pool`; [test-targets.md](../test-targets.md#the-targets) adds the proofs of `test-word`; and the design's Scheduled now says that a revisit ends when an answer is accepted or the question is closed as moot. Three behaviours are stated until the Hive Mind rules on them and are listed as open points in [word.md](../word.md#left-undecided): the moot close as the one explicit supersession of an unanswered card, a released, completed or moot question as final, and a revision of a deferred question keeping its deferral.

It is a minor change by the rule of [spec/README.md](../README.md#changing-a-contract): it adds two record schemas, twenty-six operation bodies, six operations of `hive-pool`, three operations of `hive-word`, three kinds of `hive-pool read`, receipt kinds, event kinds and error codes, each of which an existing reader can ignore, and it moves no ownership. Every record and declaration valid under the version before is valid under 1.19. It asks of code already built one addition, the contract library's handoff for a command with no store, which the lane that builds it adds; until then the library's contract version constant stays at the last version whose demands the build meets.
