# Version 1.11

Version 1.11 records, against the execution place [pool.md](../pool.md#the-execution-place) defines, the design's rules for a Hunt placed in a Claude cloud session, as [Claude in the Void](../../docs/design.md#claude-in-the-void), [Render and deliver the Imperative](../../docs/design.md#render-and-deliver-the-imperative) and [Synapse on the operator's machine](../../docs/design.md#synapse-on-the-operators-machine) now state them: the launch passes the Hunt's class as the explicit model and effort of the effective profile resolved for it, and a launch that names no model is refused, never left to the vendor's default; the Imperative's Definition of done requires of the session a record of its own model and effort in a status file on its branch; collection verifies the collected commits against that record and reads a recorded model or effort that differs from the class, or a record that is missing, as a finding and never as a clean collection; and nothing the session reports of its own checks stands for a check bound to the operator's machine. The Pool records the place and nothing of the model, which stays the resolver's, and the contradiction check that keeps a harness of another vendor out of this place is unchanged.

No schema changes: `claude-cloud-session` keeps its name and its meaning in `records/pool.item.v1`, the generation entry records the place requested and the outcome the closer names as before, and the comparison and its finding are the dispatcher's, in a collection receipt stage 4 defines. It is a minor change by the rule of [spec/README.md](../README.md#changing-a-contract) and the precedent of 1.7 and 1.10: it adds to the prose of one contract what a reader of the Pool's records can ignore, and no member, no operation, no code, no record kind, no package and no dependency; every record and declaration valid under 1.10 is valid under 1.11; and it asks nothing of code already built, so the library's contract version constant does not move.
