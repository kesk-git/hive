# Version 1.30

Version 1.30 registers the six identifier prefixes the Pool mints at stage 2, `adm_`, `spec_`, `rule_`, `hold_`, `mem_` and `migr_`, as rows of the table in [identities.md](../identities.md#identifier-form) and as constants of the identity library, where each mints and parses and agrees with its pattern in [`records/pool.item.v1`](../schemas/records/pool.item.v1.schema.json). It supplies the accepted examples and the refused examples of sixteen schemas, the records `pool.item` and `pool.hold` and the request and response bodies of `hive-pool` `add`, `update`, `transition`, `hold`, `release`, `open-generation` and `close-generation`, and removes their sixteen deferral rows from [test-targets.md](../test-targets.md#schema-fixture-obligations).

It is a minor change by the rule of [spec/README.md](../README.md#changing-a-contract): it registers prefixes by the process identities.md states and adds no operation, no record kind, no schema, no package and no dependency; every record, response and declaration valid under 1.29 is valid under 1.30; no schema changes; and no package declaration or ownership changes, so the library's contract version constant does not move.
