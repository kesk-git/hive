# Version 1.15

Version 1.15 states, in the design and in the contracts that own each part, the answers to the final open points of the review of what Hive will be.

The dispatch matrix is one record of `dispatch` in the home, plain JSON and readable with any tool. It is written only through The Swarm's editor or through a sentence to the Hive, each change a policy request with its Accepted and its Applied receipt; the policy resolver reads it at every dispatch and at the launch of every fix round's fixer; and a worker is launched on its resolved model, effort and place and never reads it. One gate entry of the matrix holds the gate's round values together: the repair budget, the escalation round with its fifth-round signal, the exhausted-budget outcome, the check bounds and the fixer's step-up, `fixer_after_round` with `after_round` 2. While the plugin is the gate's executor its own configuration holds the same step-up, and Hive compares the two at every Synapse start and holds the route where they differ, editing neither. [decisions.md](../decisions.md#left-to-the-stage-that-needs-it) leaves the record's name and its schema to stage 4, and [records.md](../records.md#the-home) names the matrix among what `dispatch/` holds.

No published attestation is required. The gate publishes its public-safe findings to a Harvest and nothing else, under the operator's own `gh` login, with no publication identity and no Spoor, and the readiness receipt on the operator's machine is the only guard of the base branch. The design states the limit that follows in [Publication](../../docs/design.md#publication-github-as-an-optional-publisher): anything that runs as the operator with `gh` signed in can merge a base the gate has not passed, and Hive's own rule is what stops it. A tighter guard, an attestation published under an identity of its own and required by a ruleset, is made only on the Hive Mind's word. The `test-synapse` row of [test-targets.md](../test-targets.md#the-targets) and the calls of `synapse` in [packages.md](../packages.md#calls-between-packages) read accordingly: the publisher resolves no Spoor, and `synapse` calls `dispatch` for the matrix's gate entry.

The design states each technical point it had left silent or unclear. A define step is a Lictor of class 1 and its builder check a fresh Lictor of class 2, after which the Fleet accepts the definition and files the Prey and their Hunts itself; a Target needs a define step exactly when no accepted specification covers its outcome; and a change to what the Hive Mind sees reaches the Hive Mind as a Review artifact with decision cards, in no other form. The dispatch axes are four, carried as an item's `placement_requested` and its three `delivery` fields, as [pool.md](../pool.md#what-an-item-carries) now words them. A Lictor whose sources are public runs in a Claude cloud session when its report can return privately, and for a public repository it stays on the operator's machine until stage 4 proves that return path. The Hive Mind watches an agent's terminal through one read-only operation of `dispatch`, over a channel local to the machine, a socket on macOS and Linux and a named pipe on Windows, which [distribution.md](../distribution.md#resident-processes-and-listeners) and [records.md](../records.md#the-home) now name, the socket being the one entry of a home that is not a plain file or directory. A project's Assimilation posture is a rule record at the project's scope, subject `assimilation.posture`, recorded only from the Hive Mind's own sentence, which [pool.md](../pool.md#rules) names and to which admission's `posture` reference points. The decision intake is defined as stage 2's next contract change, with an answer's source recorded and no proof of presence asked. The disposition note on a decision card and the Lictor's reply to a review round are named apart. The Shadow's cadence is a configuration record of its own, which no temporary rule changes. The fold runs inside every stow and before every checkpoint, a rule write re-renders the projections of its scope in the same transaction, `check` runs on the Shadow's reconciliation backstop, and Hive's own maintenance cycle is left with a nightly archive and its report. A Target's progress shows as each Hunt's position on a fixed track, with no percentage and no finish time. The three published records are written in the shapes their viewer already reads, and instructions and skills are read-only parts of the artefact.

A rule has a kind, `operating` or `product`, and an operating rule a tier, `always` or `subject`, a subject that is a record of the Pool with the steps that load it, and a rule line of at most 300 characters; a product ruling made before its work has a specification is a rule record that names the Target or the stage it governs and renders no line. A supervisor's `always` projection is named at its launch, a subject's projection arrives in the response of the command that performs the step, and a worker receives its rules in its Imperative alone. [pool.md](../pool.md#rules) states these and [leaves undecided](../pool.md#left-undecided) the members and the subject record, so the schema of a rule stands as it is.

Three points keep the testing rules of 1.13 buildable. At a Hunt's landing the Windows and Linux runner checks are outside the rule that every current check is green: a red one is a finding on the Hunt, and the Hunt lands, which rule 9 of [test-targets.md](../test-targets.md#rules-for-every-target) now states. A red scheduled run of main that stands writes a receipt on the project, which `hive-assimilate` and `hive-assimilate-local` read and refuse every landing that completes a Target on, until a green run at a head at or after the fix replaces it. And a project's [test contract](../test-targets.md#a-projects-test-contract) carries declarations only: a test's measured time, its last result and its re-runs are receipts in the home and no longer a field of the committed file, since a measurement records a run. A worker's rulings travel in its completion report, the review packet and the risk its Harvest's body states.

No schema's shape changes: the description of a rule's `subject` names the posture's key. The version adds record kinds described in prose, each with the stage that gives it a schema: the Shadow's cadence record, the receipt of a red scheduled run and the receipts of a test's measurements, which [decisions.md](../decisions.md#left-to-the-stage-that-needs-it) lists, and the subject record of the Pool. It is a minor change by the rule of [spec/README.md](../README.md#changing-a-contract) and the precedent of 1.7 and 1.10 to 1.14: it changes the prose of the contracts and the design and the text of schema descriptions, adds new kinds, which an existing reader can ignore, and adds no member, no operation, no package and no dependency. It takes one field, `measured_seconds`, out of the test contract, a record kind that 1.13 described in prose only and whose schema enters with stage 5: no schema of that kind exists, no file of it has been written and no reader of it is built, so no record valid under 1.14 becomes invalid, and every record and declaration valid under 1.14 is valid under 1.15. It asks nothing of code already built, so the library's contract version constant does not move.
