# Version 1.23

Version 1.23 gives Synapse one reviewer per review round and one fixer, named by the dispatch matrix's gate entry: the reviewer, its configured after-round member, the fixer and its configured after-round member. A second look after a fix is the next review round, subject to the existing verified non-production continuity from round 3. The scheduled full run of main remains tests and reporting, with no review step.

The reviewer's first-choice counterpart follows the rule for every matrix class: it comes from another vendor and is taken only under a recorded temporary rule or when the first choice is exhausted, with the resolved selection and its reason recorded. Gate policy names roles through the matrix rather than naming a reviewer vendor or model. Local placement, adapter facts, the same-vendor fixer step-up, fix custody and the other gate obligations remain unchanged.

The design, dispatch and Synapse contracts, and test-target obligations require one selected reviewer result per round, including in route qualification, readiness, candidate publication and Assimilation. This supersedes the two-reviewer policy retained by earlier versions. References follow the renamed Synapse section. The operator's artifact-review files and their schemas do not describe Synapse reviewer results and remain unchanged.

This is a minor revision of prospective workflow contracts: existing readers can ignore the clarified selection and result requirements, no ownership moves and no accepted record shape changes. Every previously valid record remains valid; no schema migration is needed. The implementation constant stays at the last version whose demands the build meets.
