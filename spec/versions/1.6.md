# Version 1.6

Version 1.6 gives a Pool item a level, `target`, `prey` or `hunt`, with the `wave` of a Target, the `parent` edge's direction, the derived `landed` fact and the error codes `pool.hierarchy` and `pool.derived_state`, and makes the four corrections the last review of the stage 2 contracts left: a lift carries its receipt whole, the legacy export suppresses no archived item, an in-flight item with no open generation returns to the queue, and freshness is compared by the rules digest. It is a minor change: it adds optional members, a transition and two error codes; every record and declaration valid under 1.5 is valid under 1.6, and so is every request but two that the corrections refuse by design, an `add` without a level for an item that is not a decision and a lift without its receipt, which could not be verified; and no stage 1 schema changed.
