# Version 1.16

Version 1.16 states, in the design and in the contracts that own each part, how Hive shows a running Hunt's progress in real time and how a Prey and a Target count it.

A Hunt's progress is its position on one fixed track of eight places, the same for every Hunt: `queued`, `launched`, `running`, `collected`, `validating`, `published`, `ready` and `landed`. Each place is reached on a record Hive already keeps, for the Hunt's current generation and, from `collected` on, its current head, and is taken away when that record stops holding; a route passes only the places it has. While a Synapse run is open the position goes one level down, to the step in flight by its name and ordinal, the fix round and the finding counts. Measured activity stands beside the position and is never added into it: a worktree's head, the commits ahead of its base, the files changed and whether it is clean, the worker's turn and its last sign of life, or for a session in the Void its pushes, reading launched, nothing pushed yet until the first. A Hunt that waits says whether it waits on the Hive Mind, on its supervisor or on something outside, which it names. A Prey counts its Hunts by position and a Target its Prey by column and its Hunts by position, along the Pool's `parent` edges at the moment of a read. In place of a forecast, the recorded range of past runs at the same place, on the same route and in the same project, is shown with the number of runs it rests on, read from the retained movement events of completed generations. Nothing is computed as a percentage, a fraction or a finish time, no worker declares a step, and no instruction enters an Imperative. The design states all of it in [Progress in real time](../../docs/design.md#progress-in-real-time), and the Pool, the task inspector, the map, the record model, the Shadow's cadence, the runtime adapters, Synapse, the plan and the evidence still required point to it.

Progress reaches the Hive Mind with no model turn. The Shadow keeps one progress record for each Hunt under `shadow/records/progress/`, replaced whenever one of its facts changes; a Hunt with no generation has none and takes its position from its Pool item. It holds places, step names, ordinals, counts, times, full revisions, labels and the identifiers of the records behind them, and no text written by anyone, no file name or path and no value. The Shadow writes one `shadow.progress.moved` event for each change of position and none for a tick. Hive's own app receives the records as a stream that its program pushes to its pages from inside the program, so no listener is opened and [distribution.md](../distribution.md#resident-processes-and-listeners) stands as it is; a terminal reads the same records once, through a read-only operation of `hive-state` that any repeater may run again. The Shadow reads a run's step, round and finding counts through `hive-synapse`, which the design's cadence table now names in place of the plugin's own status command, since only `synapse` runs the plugin; and it reads a worktree's facts through the Spawning Pool's `observe`, whose body stage 3 leaves room for.

The version adds a record kind and an event kind of `shadow`, both described in prose: the progress record, whose schema stage 4 gives and stage 5 extends with the validation detail, and `shadow.progress.moved`, which stage 4 registers with its body schema. [decisions.md](../decisions.md#left-to-the-stage-that-needs-it) lists them with the read-only operation of `hive-state` and the call from `shadow` to `spawning-pool` that stage 4 adds to the Shadow's declaration; [records.md](../records.md#the-home) names the record among what `shadow/` holds; [pool.md](../pool.md#reading-the-pool) lists what the Shadow reads of the Pool for it; and the `test-shadow`, `test-synapse` and `test-ui` rows of [test-targets.md](../test-targets.md#the-targets) gain the proofs of the stages that build it.

It is a minor change by the rule of [spec/README.md](../README.md#changing-a-contract) and the precedent of 1.7 and 1.10 to 1.15: it changes the prose of the design and the contracts and adds new kinds, which an existing reader can ignore, and it adds no schema, no member, no operation, no package and no dependency and changes no package declaration. Every record and declaration valid under 1.15 is valid under 1.16, and it asks nothing of code already built, so the library's contract version constant does not move.
