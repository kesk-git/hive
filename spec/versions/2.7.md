# Version 2.7

Version 2.7 registers the two error codes of refusals that [The Imperative](../pool.md#the-imperative) and the request schema of `hive-imperative deliver` already require, and that the error table had no code for.

`pool.projection_stale` names the refusal of a rendering whose embedded subject projection is stale: the section already says that a subject whose projection is stale refuses the rendering, as a conflicted one does with `pool.rule_conflict`. `hive-imperative render` computes the projection's freshness at the instant of rendering, as `hive-memory check` and `hive-pool read` do, and the refusal's detail names the projection and its stale sources. A bound subject that has no projection is not found, and a conflicted one stays `pool.rule_conflict`.

`pool.generation_not_open` names the refusal of a delivery to a generation the item does not have or that is not open: the request schema of `deliver` already says the generation must be open on the item. The refusal's detail names the item and the generation. It is the converse of `pool.generation_open`, which names a generation that is open when none may be.

No schema's shape changes and no operation, member or record is added. This is a minor change under [Changing a contract](../README.md#changing-a-contract): it adds two error codes, and every request and record valid before the change is valid after it.
